01 / THE IDEA

A video model can do more than predict.

Future observations provide dense supervision for robot control. RAP-WAM asks how a joint predictor can make that supervision more useful—through its own predictions and its responses to actions.

The base is adapted directly from a video-pretrained backbone using target-task demonstrations, without intermediate pretraining on external embodied data. Agent-guided refinement then retains task-specific improvements.
